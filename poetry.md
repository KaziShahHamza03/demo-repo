# Sun and Sky

The sun spills gold across the morning blue,  
And wakes the clouds to wander, soft and slow.  
The sky holds every shade the day can brew,  
While warm light paints the hills below.

At dusk, the sun sinks gently out of sight,  
The sky turns rose, then violet, then deep.  
And stars appear to keep the dark alight,  
As earth and all its dreaming settle into sleep.
